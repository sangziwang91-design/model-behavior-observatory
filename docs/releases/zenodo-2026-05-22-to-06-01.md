## Release summary

This release note records three additional public Zenodo records in the Model Behavior Observatory public research surface.

These records extend the earlier EC-EpC, behavioral cartography, rule-layer, interaction-ecology, structural-integrity, convergence-inertia, and medical-AI audit records with a more explicit focus on evaluation-environment effects, output-layer behavioral scoring, and cross-model behavioral measurement.

The release remains intentionally bounded. It records public DOI entries, public-safe summaries, and methodological roles without exposing private audit procedures, prompt-routing logic, scoring thresholds, intervention templates, or unpublished control workflows.

## Claim ceiling

These records should be read under a bounded claim ceiling:

- exploratory or pilot-scale status unless otherwise specified;
- public-safe method framing rather than validated benchmark release;
- no model-ranking claim;
- no population-level prevalence claim;
- no direct inference about hidden model internals;
- no claim that the public materials constitute a complete operational safety or control system;
- stronger claims require external replication, independent scoring, and broader model/task coverage.

---

## Public-safe boundaries

The public release intentionally includes:

- DOI-indexed public records;
- high-level framework descriptions;
- dataset-level documentation;
- reproducibility-oriented materials where released;
- summary-level methodological framing;
- limitations and validation requirements.

The public release intentionally excludes:

- private audit prompts;
- private scoring weights and thresholds;
- prompt-routing logic;
- intervention templates;
- unreleased validation workflows;
- reverse-engineerable control procedures;
- raw private logs not approved for public release.
