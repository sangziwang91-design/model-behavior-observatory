# May 2026 Zenodo Release Note

## Model Behavior Observatory public record index

This release note records the **five May 2026 Zenodo records** associated with the bounded public research surface of the Model Behavior Observatory.

Two earlier April 2026 records are not duplicated here as May records. They remain the established entry-point records for the EC-EpC gap and behavioral cartography line:

- Wang, S. (2026). *Auditing Epistemological Credibility in LLM Self-Reports: A Dual-Axis Behavioral Framework and Pilot Evidence*. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19879788
- Wang, S. (2026). *Cross-Model Behavioral Cartography: A Pilot Dataset Summary for LLM Self-Report Reliability Research*. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19881753

The May 2026 materials are released as hypothesis-generating preprints, specification notes, and pilot dataset records. They are intended to support external citation, replication planning, public-safe discussion, and cautious methodological development. They should not be interpreted as validated benchmarks, regulatory-grade standards, population-level prevalence estimates, operational control procedures, or claims about internal model mechanisms.

This index is intentionally conservative: it records public DOI entries and their limited roles without presenting the full internal research system, private audit workflow, scoring logic, or unpublished control architecture.

## Claim ceiling

All May 2026 records should be read under a bounded claim ceiling:

- exploratory or hypothesis-generating status;
- single-researcher or limited-access observations unless otherwise stated;
- no claim of validated benchmark status;
- no claim of population-level prevalence;
- no model-ranking claim;
- no direct inference about hidden model internals;
- no claim that these records constitute a complete operational system;
- external replication and independent scoring remain required for stronger claims.

## Public-safe boundaries

The public release intentionally includes:

- high-level concepts and definitions;
- DOI-indexed records;
- public-safe summaries;
- pilot-level evidence descriptions;
- methodological limitations;
- replication-oriented framing.

The public release intentionally excludes:

- private audit prompts;
- full task packs;
- scoring weights and thresholds;
- operational trigger templates;
- reverse-engineerable control procedures;
- raw private logs not already approved for public release;
- personal or institutional data beyond public citation metadata.
